About

    

      
> Profile

      
Vishu Aggarwal is a novelist and clarinetist. His two novels, The Aurora and The Beginning of Memory, are on Amazon, and he's writing a third called No Trouble. He composes his own music, including Chandni Raahein, Night of the Golden Lamps and Rangon Ka Dhamaka. He's also coding Aries, an app where a crew of AI agents runs a business, and building a bean-to-cup coffee machine at home.
